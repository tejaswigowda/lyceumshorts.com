Why cells are small

Claim

For cubes, surface area divided by volume is 6, 3 and 2 for sides 1, 2 and 3, falling as 1 / side, so a bigger cell has less surface for each bit of interior.

Demonstration. Shows the relationship in particular cases. It does not prove it in general.

Assumptions

  • cells are idealised cubes built from an exact grid of unit cubes
  • ratios are per unit-cube width; SA / V ∝ 1 / side holds in any units
  • the folded membranes (cristae, alveoli) illustrate the idea; they are not measurements of real membranes

How it was checked

Not formally verified. The film shows the relationship; no check was run against a formula.

Transcript

Captions are on by default in the player. Times link to that moment in the film.

  1. 0:01 Why are cells so small? It comes down to geometry. Start with a cube, one unit on a side. #
  2. 0:10 Six faces of one square each: surface 6. Volume 1. Surface to volume: 6. #
  3. 0:19 Double the side: each face is 4 squares, so surface 24. Volume 8. Surface to volume: 3. #
  4. 0:29 Triple it: each face is 9 squares, so surface 54. Volume 27. Surface to volume: 2. #
  5. 0:39 Surface grows with the square of the side, volume with the cube. So the bigger it is, the less skin it has for each bit of inside. #
  6. 0:49 A cell lives through its skin: food and oxygen come in, waste goes out. Too big, and the middle starves. #
  7. 0:58 So cells divide. Cut the big cube into slabs, then bars, then 27 small cubes. #
  8. 1:06 The volume is still 27, but the surface is now 162. Surface to volume: 6 again. #
  9. 1:16 Life uses this trick everywhere. Inside a mitochondrion, folds called cristae pack in membrane. #
  10. 1:24 In the lungs, millions of tiny alveoli give a surface of about 70 square metres. #
  11. 1:31 Small, or folded: that is how living things get enough surface. #
  12. 1:36 Made with Strata. #

What the colours mean

The film uses colour to carry meaning. Here is what each one distinguishes.

aqua
the unit cubes that make each cell.
pink outline
the skin: the surface of each cube, drawn as a see-through shell.
gold
the single cube in the middle of the side-3 cube, the one farthest from the skin; also the numbers and the biology notes.
lilac
the folded membrane in the last scenes.
white
the readout of side, surface area, volume and their ratio.
