Squares on a right triangle #

Maths: Pythagorean theorem · intro · 2:09 · Demonstration

Claim #

On a right triangle, the squares on the two legs together cover exactly the square on the hypotenuse: 3² + 4² = 5², shown with tiles, then again for 5-12-13 and 8-15-17.

Demonstration. Shows the relationship in particular cases. It does not prove it in general.

Assumptions #

  • the triangles are exact right triangles with whole-number sides (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17)
  • lengths are counted in tiles: a tile is a 1 × 1 × 1 cube
  • three cases are counted; the film does not show the theorem for every right triangle

How it was checked #

Not formally verified. The film shows the relationship; no check was run against a formula.

What the colours mean #

The film uses colour to carry meaning. Here is what each one distinguishes.

coral
the square on the shorter leg, built from tiles (9 tiles for the 3-4-5 triangle).
aqua
the square on the longer leg (16 tiles for 3-4-5). Coral and aqua tiles together fill the square on the hypotenuse.
gold
the right triangle itself.
white
the numbers and equations that count the tiles.
