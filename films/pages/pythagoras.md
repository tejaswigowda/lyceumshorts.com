<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<p class="claim-text">On a right triangle, the squares on the two legs together cover exactly the square on the hypotenuse: 3² + 4² = 5², shown with tiles, then again for 5-12-13 and 8-15-17.</p>
<p class="small"><strong>Demonstration.</strong> Shows the relationship in particular cases. It does not prove it in general.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h2 id="assumptions">Assumptions</h2>
<ul class="assumptions">
<li>the triangles are exact right triangles with whole-number sides (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17)</li>
<li>lengths are counted in tiles: a tile is a 1 × 1 × 1 cube</li>
<li>three cases are counted; the film does not show the theorem for every right triangle</li>
</ul>
</section>

<h2 id="checked">How it was checked</h2>
<p>Not formally verified. The film shows the relationship; no check was run against a formula.</p>

<h3>What the colours mean</h3>
<p>The film uses colour to carry meaning. Here is what each one distinguishes.</p>
<dl class="colours">
<dt>coral</dt><dd>the square on the shorter leg, built from tiles (9 tiles for the 3-4-5 triangle).</dd>
<dt>aqua</dt><dd>the square on the longer leg (16 tiles for 3-4-5). Coral and aqua tiles together fill the square on the hypotenuse.</dd>
<dt>gold</dt><dd>the right triangle itself.</dd>
<dt>white</dt><dd>the numbers and equations that count the tiles.</dd>
</dl>
