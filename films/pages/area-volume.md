<section>
<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<p class="claim-text">For cubes, surface area divided by volume is 6, 3 and 2 for sides 1, 2 and 3, falling as 1 / side, so a bigger cell has less surface for each bit of interior.</p>
<p class="small"><strong>Demonstration.</strong> Shows the relationship in particular cases. It does not prove it in general.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h2 id="assumptions">Assumptions</h2>
<ul class="assumptions">
<li>cells are idealised cubes built from an exact grid of unit cubes</li>
<li>ratios are per unit-cube width; SA / V ∝ 1 / side holds in any units</li>
<li>the folded membranes (cristae, alveoli) illustrate the idea; they are not measurements of real membranes</li>
</ul>
</section>
