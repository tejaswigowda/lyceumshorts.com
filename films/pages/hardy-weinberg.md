<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<p class="claim-text">With random mating, one generation of offspring comes in the proportions p² + 2pq + q², drawn as a square, and the allele frequencies p and q are unchanged afterwards.</p>
<p class="small"><strong>Demonstration.</strong> Shows the relationship in particular cases. It does not prove it in general.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h2 id="assumptions">Assumptions</h2>
<ul class="assumptions">
<li>one gene with two alleles, A and a</li>
<li>random mating in a large (effectively infinite) population</li>
<li>no mutation, no selection, no migration, no drift</li>
<li>the film shows one round with p = 3/5 and q = 2/5 (a 5 × 5 square of tiles); it is one worked case, not the general law</li>
</ul>
</section>
<section>
<h2 id="checked">How it was checked</h2>
<ul>
<li>the 30 A tokens and 20 a tokens span the same extents as the p and q rulers: 50 samples. Read back from the loaded scene and equal to the last digit</li>
</ul>
