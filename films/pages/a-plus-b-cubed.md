<h2 id="claim">Claim</h2>
<p class="claim-text">A cube of side a + b, cut at a along each edge, falls into eight blocks (one a³, three a²b, three ab², one b³), so (a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³ for positive lengths a and b.</p>
<p class="small"><strong>Derivation.</strong> An argument that holds for every case its assumptions cover.</p>
</section>
<section>
<h2 id="assumptions">Assumptions</h2>
<ul class="assumptions">
<li>a and b are positive lengths, so the cube can be cut at a along each edge</li>
<li>the lengths are symbolic: there are no units, and a and b are drawn at one fixed ratio the identity does not depend on</li>
</ul>
</section>
