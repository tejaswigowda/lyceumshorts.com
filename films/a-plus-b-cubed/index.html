Claim #

A cube of side a + b, cut at a along each edge, falls into eight blocks (one a³, three a²b, three ab², one b³), so (a + b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³ for positive lengths a and b.

Derivation. An argument that holds for every case its assumptions cover.

Assumptions #

  • a and b are positive lengths, so the cube can be cut at a along each edge
  • the lengths are symbolic: there are no units, and a and b are drawn at one fixed ratio the identity does not depend on

How it was checked #

Not formally verified. The film shows the relationship; no check was run against a formula.

What the colours mean #

The film uses colour to carry meaning. Here is what each one distinguishes.

coral
the one a × a × a block, and the term a³. The side a on the ruler is coral too.
gold
the three slabs a × a × b, and the term 3a²b.
violet
the three bars a × b × b, and the term 3ab².
aqua
the one b × b × b block, and the term b³. The side b on the ruler is aqua too.
pale blue
the three planes that cut the cube, one per axis.
