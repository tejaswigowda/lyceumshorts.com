Contribute

A submission is a pull request.

What the pull request contains

  1. An entry in lyceum.json. It is the only place a film is described; the pages are generated from it.
  2. The scene, in a public repository, named in the entry's scene field.
  3. The tutorial that builds the scene, linked from the entry.
  4. The render under renders/<id>/, produced by scripts/package-render.mjs from a Strata offline master.

The bar

  • The tutorial meets the tutorial policy of the editor: it builds the film from an empty scene, and its contract states units, assumptions, whether the render was run, and that every object is named and classed.
  • Claim stated: one sentence.
  • Assumptions named: a visible list.
  • Status honest: demonstration unless the film really derives the result for every case its assumptions cover.
  • Checks reported where you ran them (samples and the largest residual). Leave checks empty if you ran none; the page will say so.
  • Colour described: if colour carries meaning, say what each colour distinguishes in colour_key.
  • Captions on every film.
  • Original or openly licensed assets only, with credits listed.

Before you open it

node scripts/generate.mjs
node scripts/check.mjs

generate.mjs rebuilds the pages from lyceum.json; check.mjs runs the site's gates (every entry complete, no unearned "proof", every segment under 20 MB, captions present, no third-party requests, colour contrast) and check.mjs --scenes compares each render with its scene's current version.
