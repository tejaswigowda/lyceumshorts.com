About Lyceum #

A commons of short educational films that ship their own source.

Anyone can host explainer videos. Here every film comes with the scene that made it and the tutorial that built it. Watch it, open the scene in an editor, change it, and read how it was put together. You do not need the editor to learn the subject; it is there if you want it.

Correctable, not just watchable #

A wrong explainer on any other platform stays wrong. The author may never see the comment, and the video cannot be edited, only replaced. Here the film is rendered from a scene file in a public repository. If a number is off or a claim is too strong, you open a pull request against the scene or against this site's lyceum.json, and the fix is reviewed in the open.

What each film says about itself #

Every film states, on its page, in plain words:

  • The claim: one sentence about what the film shows.
  • The assumptions: what it takes for granted. No air resistance, random mating, rigid parts: they are listed, not buried.
  • Its status: a demonstration shows a relationship in particular cases; a derivation argues it for every case its assumptions cover. Nothing here is called a proof unless it is one.
  • How it was checked: for example, that the ball's position matches the closed form to a stated residual. If nothing was checked, the page says so.

What this is not #

Not a video gallery, not a course platform, and not documentation for the editor. The editor's own tutorials serve people learning the tool. These films serve people learning the subject.

Privacy #

This site makes no requests to any other origin: no analytics, no fonts, no embeds, no trackers. The video player is served from here too.
